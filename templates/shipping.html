Shipping & Returns

1. Delivery Times

All pieces are produced in limited runs. Ready-to-wear items generally ship within 3-5 business days. Custom or made-to-order pieces require 4-6 weeks for production before dispatch.

2. Shipping Costs

We offer complimentary standard shipping on all domestic orders. Expedited shipping and international delivery rates are calculated at checkout based on destination and package weight.

3. Returns Policy

We accept returns on unworn, unwashed items with all original tags attached within 14 days of delivery. Custom or personalized pieces are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged.

4. How to Return

To initiate a return, please contact our studio via email. We will provide a return authorization and a shipping label. A return processing fee may apply and will be deducted from your refund.

5. Damaged Items

If you receive an item that is damaged or defective, please contact us immediately upon receipt with photographs of the defect so we can arrange an exchange or refund.
